# Local SEO manual checklist

Use the existing Simmply Perfect business identity. These are manual actions, not completed account changes.

- Verify ownership of the existing Google Business Profile before editing it. Search for the existing listing linked in the website footer; do not create a duplicate.
- Confirm the customer-facing business name, actual public phone, email, website, service area and address with the owner. Website contact copy currently gives only a general location; do not guess a street address or infer it from a Maps viewport.
- Choose the most accurate primary business category from Google's currently available options based on the principal real business activity. Add secondary categories only for services actually provided.
- Add genuine windows, doors, metal fabrication, interiors and renovation services/products that the business offers. Verify specifications, warranties, prices and service boundaries before publication.
- Upload genuine project photos with permission and accurate descriptions. Do not label stock images as completed projects.
- Request honest reviews from real customers without incentives, filtering or invented reviews. Respond constructively; do not add unverified ratings to website schema.
- Audit existing business directories for consistent name, address and phone. Correct duplicates and obsolete listings.
- Seek relevant links from actual suppliers, professional memberships and local project partners. Avoid purchased or artificial link networks.
- In Search Console, verify the canonical HTTPS domain property, submit /sitemap.xml and inspect representative service URLs. Check Google-selected canonicals, rendering resources and Page Indexing after deployment.
- Keep /book-service noindex; confirm /affiliate and /partner remain indexable. Inspect robots.txt on the deployed site after caches refresh.
- Run Google's Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator on deployed pages. Valid schema does not guarantee a search enhancement.
- Review Search Console queries and actual enquiries to prioritize future articles. Do not build neighbourhood pages without distinct, verified local content.
- Collect mobile field Core Web Vitals and test real enquiry/booking flows before further performance changes.

Guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start
