## Additional intent decisions

- Window/glass repair, replacement, cleaning, mosquito mesh, installation and maintenance are present in homepage service cards: use the homepage's existing /#general-services section for service discovery, then the existing booking CTA for transactions. The fragment is not a separate indexable URL.
- Glass fabrication/installation intent maps to the existing Windows & Doors material section and relevant homepage glass service. Steel gates/grills intent maps to Metal Works. No new glass landing page is needed.
- Energy efficiency, acoustic insulation, maintenance, sliding versus casement and tinted versus reflective glass questions belong in Articles where the existing content supports them. Do not claim a specific acoustic rating, lifespan, glazing specification or warranty without verified product data.
- Do not target “best”, manufacturer/dealer status or nearby neighbourhood names merely because they appear in the requested keyword list. Add only verified, useful information.
- No search-volume counts, guaranteed rankings, new testimonials, locations or FAQ answers were invented.
